03 — AI Core

The intelligence layer behind every module.

A shared AI platform prevents duplicated models and creates a consistent intelligence layer across the university.

🔮 Prediction

GPA, failure risk, graduation delay, demand and transport ETA.

🎯 Recommendation

Courses, careers, internships, resources, teams and interventions.

💬 NLP / RAG

University assistant, feedback analysis, document Q&A and research support.

👁️ Computer Vision

Occupancy and selected campus monitoring use cases with privacy controls.

🧩 Optimization
